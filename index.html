Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

When is UK:RP Edinburgh releasing?
Our current targeted launch window is set between Late 2029 and Late 2032. This gives our development team ample time to build high-quality Edinburgh locations and custom Roblox scripts.
How do I join Police Scotland or Emergency Services?
You can join our official Discord server and fill out the faction application templates pinned in the applications channel.
Will the game be playable on mobile/Chromebook/console?
Yes! We are optimizing UI and vehicle controls so players across PC, Mobile, and Console devices can join seamlessly.
