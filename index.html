About Our Community

Welcome to UK:RP Edinburgh! Based in Scotland's iconic capital city, our server delivers immersive, highly realistic Scottish emergency response and civilian roleplay. Whether you want to enforce law and order, deliver life-saving medical care, tackle major fires, or build a business empire, Edinburgh has a place for you.
